# Vegetable Garden Plan

## Context
Two raised beds (33" × 108" = 2.75 ft × 9 ft each, ~24.75 sq ft per bed / ~49.5 sq ft total) oriented north–south, placed side by side with no path between them. The garden faces northeast. **Bed 1 is on the west**, closer to a southwest tree that filters afternoon light on its south portion; it has a permanent trellis on its north short end. **Bed 2 is on the east**, full sun all day. Location: **USDA Zone 7b** (last frost ~April 20, first frost ~October 24). Goal: maximize productivity across spring, summer, and fall using smart placement, succession planting, and vertical growing.

## Sun & Shade Analysis

| Location | Light | Best use |
|---|---|---|
| Bed 2 (east, full) | Full sun all day | Sun-hungry crops: tomatoes, melons, zucchini |
| Bed 1 north / trellis zone | Full sun | Cucumbers on trellis (trellis doesn't shade the bed — sun comes from S/E) |
| Bed 1 south | Partial PM shade | Cilantro, dill, bok choy — afternoon shade slows heat-triggered bolting |
| Both beds north ends | Max exposure | Always place tallest plants here |

**Core rule:** Tall plants always go at the north end — sun arrives from south/east, not north.

## SPRING

**Crops:** Bok choy, cilantro, dill, Swiss chard, kale, sugar snap peas
**Cucumbers:** Plant at last frost on Bed 1 trellis; they carry into summer.

### Succession Strategy

| Crop | Rounds | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Cilantro | 4 | Bolts fast in warmth |
| Dill | 4 | Bolts moderately fast |
| Bok choy | 4 | Bolts in heat |
| Kale | 2 | Cut-and-come-again; bolt-resistant |
| Swiss chard | 2 | Cut-and-come-again; very long producing |
| Sugar snap peas | 2 | S1 on Bed 1 trellis (pull May 10 for cucumbers); S2 on Bed 2 new trellis |

**Sow trigger:** When the current batch shows first flower buds (bolting signal), immediately sow the next round in the next available strip.

### Summer Bed Layout

**Bed 1** (west, trellis):

```
NORTH
├── 0–24"   : 2× Persian cucumbers — west & center of trellis zone, on permanent trellis
│             PERMANENT: Thyme (1 sq ft) — northeast corner (east ~12" of this zone)
├── 24–48"  : Cherry tomato — single-stake, center/east of bed; prune all suckers weekly
├── 48–78"  : 1× Thai chili
└── 78–108" : Jalapeño — south end, tolerates partial PM shade from SW tree
SOUTH
```

**Bed 2** (east, full sun — all the heavy-feeders):

```
NORTH
├── 0–27"   : Roma tomato — staked/tied to north trellis, single or double leader; plant center/east of strip
│             PERMANENT: Oregano (1 sq ft) — northwest corner (west ~12" of this zone)
├── 27–60"  : 2× Minnesota midget melons — vertical stakes/string, train upward aggressively
│             PERMANENT: Rosemary (0.5 sq ft) — west side of strip
└── 60–108" : 1× Zucchini — allow to sprawl south over bed edge
SOUTH
```

### ⚠ Summer Space Alerts

1. **Zucchini** will exceed the 2.75 ft bed width — plan for leaves to sprawl over the south edge.
2. **Cherry tomato must be kept to a single leader** — remove all suckers weekly, or it will overwhelm a 2.75 ft bed.
3. **Minnesota midget melons** — vines reach 3–4 ft; train up a string trellis tied to stakes. Support developing fruits in mesh bags or old pantyhose attached to the stake.
4. **When cucumbers finish** (likely Aug–Sep), Bed 1's north trellis zone frees ~5.5 sq ft — transition immediately to fall kale or late sugar snaps.

## FALL

Same crops as spring. Succession every 2 weeks, working backward from first frost.

### Fall Succession Calendar

Zone 7b: first frost ~October 24.

| Round | **Zone 7b** | Crops to sow |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Jul 31** | Kale, chard, sugar snaps, bok choy — start in Bed 2 where zucchini/melons wind down |
| F2 | **Aug 14** | Cilantro, dill, bok choy |
| F3 | **Aug 28** | Cilantro, dill, bok choy, kale |
| F4 | **Sep 11** | Cilantro, dill (shorter crops that can still mature before frost) |

**Fall transition notes:**
- Start F1 in the south end of Bed 2 first — zucchini finishes earliest.
- Tomatoes and peppers stay productive through fall; don't pull until frost.
- When cucumbers clear Bed 1 trellis, plant fall kale there as a bonus strip.

## Crop-Specific Notes

- **Sugar snap peas (6ft variety)**: Must go at the north end of beds — 6ft vines shade everything south of them. Use Bed 1 permanent trellis for S1; use new Bed 2 north trellis for S2. **S1 hard deadline: pull by May 10** to hand off the Bed 1 trellis to cucumbers. Small stakes will not work for this variety.
- **Persian baby cucumbers**: Direct sow at last frost date, or start indoors 3 weeks early. Train every few days to the trellis to keep within the 2.75 ft width.
- **Minnesota midget melons**: Need pollinators — the flowering cilantro and dill in adjacent beds will attract bees. Hand-pollinate if fruit set is poor (use a small paintbrush flower-to-flower).
- **Zucchini**: One plant is plenty — it will massively overproduce. Remove aging lower leaves to improve airflow and give neighboring plants access to light.
- **Roma tomato**: Semi-determinate; relatively self-limiting. Still prune to 1–2 leaders.
- **Cherry tomato**: Indeterminate and aggressive. Single-leader staking is non-negotiable in this bed width.
- **Thai chili + jalapeño**: Heat lovers, low maintenance, last plants producing in fall. The jalapeño tolerates the SW tree's partial afternoon shade well.

**Permanent Herbs** (year-round; do not pull at season transitions)
- **Thyme** (Bed 1, NE trellis zone): Low-growing perennial (6–12" tall). Thrives in full sun; no competition with cucumber roots. Trim stems by no more than one-third at once. Flowers attract pollinators in late spring. Hardy to Zone 5 — no winter protection needed.
- **Rosemary** (Bed 2, Strip 2, west side): Upright perennial — will eventually reach 2–4 ft tall and 2–3 ft wide. Trim back west/south edges each spring to keep within its 0.5 sq ft footprint and prevent shading chard and melons. Borderline hardy in Zone 7b — mulch base in December if temps drop below 10°F.
- **Oregano** (Bed 2, NW corner, Strip 1): Spreading perennial (6–18" tall, can spread 18–24"). Trim firmly to its 1 sq ft footprint each spring and after flowering to prevent encroachment into kale/melon space. Flowers are excellent pollinator magnets for adjacent melons and peppers.

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] Before Mar 28 (S2 sow): Install trellis on north short end of Bed 2.
- [ ] May 10: Pull S1 sugar snap peas from Bed 1 trellis to clear it for cucumbers (plant May 12).
- [ ] Before S1: Mark succession strips on each bed with stakes or string.
- [ ] Zone check: Confirm your exact last frost date and shift all dates accordingly.
- [ ] Bolt tracking: Keep a simple log of when each batch flowers — that's the sow-next cue.
- [ ] May bed audit: Before transplanting summer crops, confirm which spring crops are still harvestable vs. ready to pull.
- [ ] Trellis load check: Confirm the Bed 1 permanent trellis can support 2 loaded cucumber vines — add horizontal strings if needed.
- [ ] Each spring: Trim rosemary and oregano back to their designated footprints before planting seasonal crops — both will attempt to spread beyond their marked boundaries.
- [ ] Early August audit: Walk both beds to identify which summer crops are winding down and where to slot in F1 fall plantings.
